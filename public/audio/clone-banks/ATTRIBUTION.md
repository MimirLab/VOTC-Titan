# Source attribution

Original Warcraft, World of Warcraft, Hearthstone and Heroes of the Storm performances are Blizzard Entertainment recordings. Community mirrors and indexes are acquisition/research sources, not official voice-cloning handoffs. This local collection does not claim ownership or new distribution rights.

Exact original URLs, original filenames, source game, available build/actor information, SHA-256 hashes and reversible edits are in every metadata.json. Unknown attribution is not promoted to a verified actor claim.

- GoA2 character definitions: https://github.com/Warcraft-GoA-Development-Team/Warcraft-Guardians-of-Azeroth-2
- WoW original-file mirror: https://thewowdb.com/sounds/
- Caption discovery catalog: https://github.com/JessePretendstoCode/TonguesOfAzeroth/blob/master/GameVoices.lua
- HotS narrow original extraction: https://github.com/HeroesToolChest/HeroesDataParser (actual source build 2.57.0.98348; exact CASC paths retained)
- Warcraft III campaign pool compiler: https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/sound-sets-from-campaign-dialog.328584/ (inspect constituent actual codecs; some .wav files contain MP3)
- Hearthstone source index: https://hearthsfx.github.io/ (only downloaded assets are claimed)
- Breeze cloning backend: https://github.com/breezeblue-ai/breeze-tts
- Unprompted transcription tooling: https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp

The user-supplied reconciled crosswalk guides identity discovery. A positive catalog mapping alone does not verify the actual clip's speaker, cleanliness or transcript. Failed acquisition routes and unverified shared compiler soundsets remain unresolved.
